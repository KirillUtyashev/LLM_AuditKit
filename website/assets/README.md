# Static assets

## Boston newspaper scan

`boston-1990-08-05.jpg` is the maintainer-supplied research scan originally named
`boston_82_8_5_1990.jpg`. The maintainer explicitly approved publishing this scan
and its saved synthetic demonstration outputs on 19 November 2026. The date is
inferred from that research filename, not a separately verified masthead.
The third-party newspaper content is not relicensed under the project's MIT
license. Other research scans and raw run directories remain excluded.

## Chicago and Birmingham newspaper scans

Added at the maintainer's request on 22 November 2026:

- `chicago-1970-05-31.jpg`: *Chicago Tribune*, 31 May 1970, printed page D8,
  from the research collection's `Classified_Ad_12_--_No_Title.jpg` in its dated
  Chicago 1970 folder. Date/title/page are visible in the ProQuest header.
  The original reproduction/permission notice is retained in full. Possession
  of a research copy does not establish unrestricted republication rights.
- `birmingham-2000-04-02.jpg`: *The Birmingham News*, 2 April 2000, page 4G,
  research filename `birmingham_84_04_02_2000.jpg`. Title/date/page are visible
  in the masthead. This is the 2000 example explicitly selected instead of 2001.

These are unmodified display copies. Neither scan is covered by the repository's
MIT license; confirm applicable permissions before further redistribution.
Raw run directories and other research material remain excluded.
