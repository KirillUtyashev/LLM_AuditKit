# Static assets

## Boston newspaper scan

`boston-1990-08-05.jpg` is the maintainer-supplied research scan originally named
`boston_82_8_5_1990.jpg`. The maintainer explicitly approved publishing this scan
and its saved synthetic demonstration outputs on 19 November 2026. The date is
inferred from that research filename, not a separately verified masthead.
The third-party newspaper content is not relicensed under the project's MIT
license. Other research scans and raw run directories remain excluded.
